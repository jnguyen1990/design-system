Notes on direction b

  • layoutTwo columns with a fixed rail. The main column answers "how am I doing"; the 330px rail holds everything that is actually a to-do. That is the structural answer to "one big single column" — the page stops being a scroll.
  • layoutThe rail is where uncategorized finally becomes actionable. Today the count is a dead number on a tile; here each item is a row with a button next to it.
  • new"Still to come" card. Recurring data already exists, so known bills that have not landed yet can be listed. Without it "left to spend" is optimistic by about $110–220 every month.
  • new"Versus august" replaces the 3-month grouped bar chart — same question, far less ink, and it names the categories that moved instead of making you compare bar heights.
  • densityOne hero number instead of twelve tiles. Everything else is subordinate to "left to spend", which is the number you actually open the app for. Supporting figures sit under a rule inside the same card.
  • keepThe doughnut survives here but shrunk into the rail, where it works as a proportion glance rather than a primary chart.
  • riskTrade-off versus direction a: more layout machinery, and the rail collapses under the main column below 1180px, so on an iPad or phone the to-dos end up at the bottom of a long scroll. Direction a stays identical at every width.
