Notes on direction c

  • structureNo tables at all. Each category is an envelope tile showing the one number that matters day to day — what is left — with the meter and the "spent of allocated" line as support. Groups become light section labels rather than cards.
  • density17 categories fit on roughly one screen instead of four stacked tables, because a tile is about 100px tall and they flow four or five across.
  • newReads as "how much room do I have in X", which is the question you ask standing in a store. The ledger directions read as "what does my plan look like", which is the question you ask once a month.
  • newAllocation banner. When a month has money still unallocated, the page opens with that and offers the two ways to fix it. On a fully allocated month the banner collapses to a single confirming line.
  • riskTrade-off: you lose column alignment entirely, so comparing allocated across categories means reading tiles, not scanning a column. Precise monthly planning is worse here; in-month checking is better. Worth considering as a second view on the budget page — a chips toggle between ledger and envelopes — rather than a replacement.
