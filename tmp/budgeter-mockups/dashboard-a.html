Notes on direction a

  • densityThree stat bands collapsed into one. "Budget overview", "Monthly insights" and "Spending forecast" repeated each other — total spending and current spending were literally the same number. Now: one row of five tiles, with .stat-subtext carrying the second-order read instead of its own card.
  • chromeMonth stepper moved into the page header. That deletes a full-width card that held two arrows, and makes the month read as page state rather than content. Same move on the budget page.
  • fixCard descriptions no longer use .stat-label. In production those render ALL-CAPS (v4 uppercases that class), so every subtitle and empty state is shouting today. They use .card-subtitle here.
  • newFixed vs variable split. Rent, insurance, childcare and internet all land on the 1st, so a naive "64% spent, 37% of the month gone" reads as alarming when it is normal. Splitting them makes the pace bar honest — variable spending is at 36% with 37% of the month elapsed.
  • newProjection uses that split too: spent to date + bills still to land + variable daily rate × days left. The current naive daily-average × 30 would project $8,161 on Sep 11 purely because rent was paid.
  • newIncome and net tile. Inflows are already in the schema but the dashboard is spend-only today.
  • swapDoughnut → horizontal bars. Rank and magnitude are readable at a glance and the legend disappears. The 3-month grouped bar chart (up to 24 bars) is replaced by the category table's "vs aug" column.
  • colorSpending is no longer red by default. Red is reserved for over-allocation, so it means something when you see it.
  • cutThe hidden "Top categories" card is deleted rather than left in the markup with display:none.
