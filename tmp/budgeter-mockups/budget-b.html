Notes on direction b

  • structureKeeps one card per group, but makes the card header do work. Today a group card header is just the group name; here it carries the group's allocated / spent / left and a group meter, so a collapsed card is still informative.
  • newGroups collapse. Bills is six rows you rarely touch — collapse it and the page is half as tall, while the header still tells you bills are 92% committed. Click any group header to try it.
  • newPer-row meters and inline editing are the same as direction a — those two changes are independent of the group layout, so they carry across whichever structure you pick.
  • keepClosest to what you have. If the card-per-group split is something you actually like — separate bills from everyday visually — this keeps it and fixes the density and the missing subtotals, rather than throwing it out.
  • riskTrade-off versus direction a: columns still do not align across cards, because each card lays out its own table. Direction a's single table is strictly better for scanning one column top to bottom; this is better for working inside one group at a time.
