Direction B — notes

  • The premise: a morning batch is usually one clean import and a couple that need work. Every draft is a summary row you can judge without opening — thumbnail, name, confidence, and the line that matters (9 ingredients · 35 min · protein + veg · 4 servings). Tick the clean one, accept it, never open it.
  • Expanding edits in place — no page load, no losing your spot, and you can compare two drafts side by side by opening both.
  • Bulk accept is the whole point, and it's the one thing the other two directions can't do. The risk is that it makes accepting-without-looking the path of least resistance, which is how junk gets into the catalog — the thing staging was supposed to prevent.
  • Costs: the source evidence has nowhere to live — there's a "view source" link but the cookbook photo is no longer beside the fields it produced. Editing inside an expanded card is cramped at any width below about 1100px.
  • Would move into the design system: the expandable .stack-row / bulk-select bar pair. Nothing else here is new.
