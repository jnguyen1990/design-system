Recipe builder — review surface

Three directions for the page where you accept what the night job drafted, plus the capture panel that feeds it. All four run on the live design system with the same three-draft fixture — one clean blog parse, one cookbook page read from a photo, one dictated dump — so they're directly comparable. Each page ends with a dashed notes card: what the direction assumes, and what it costs.

The three directions

They differ on one axis: how much the source material stays in front of you while you edit.

direction a
Source beside draft
One draft at a time. The cookbook photo or raw dictation is pinned on the left while you edit on the right. The only direction where you can actually catch a misread.
direction b
The whole batch at once
Every draft a summary row you can judge without opening; expand to edit in place. Tick the clean one and accept it blind. Fastest, and the only one with bulk accept.
direction c
Inbox
Rail of drafts, editable pane, j/k and ⌘↵. Scales to twenty photos on a Sunday; mostly empty space at three.
shared
Capture panel
The /recipes end — link / photo / paste tabs, the queue, and the badge card that tells you drafts are waiting. Identical in all three directions.

What's already settled

From the planning questions — these aren't up for re-litigation in this round, they're the frame.

What it is
A nightly job on the mini that drafts recipes. Sibling of the postings sweep.
Intake
URLs, photos, free text — plus an MCP tool so you can queue from a Claude chat.
Autonomy
Drafts only. The job never creates a recipe; you accept.
Editing
Inline-editable before accepting — not accept-then-fix.
Failures
Always draft something, flagged, rather than leaving an error line.
Cadence
02:00 nightly, with a run-now button on base /jobs.
Rotation
Accepted recipes join at biweekly, unchanged. Review is the gate.
Macros
Passed through when the source states them; never estimated.
Where it lives
/recipes/review, reached from a badge. Nav stays at six slots.

What I still need from you

Beyond picking a direction.

1
Field-level uncertainty — keep it? All three directions tint the fields the agent guessed at and say why underneath, instead of stamping the whole draft "low confidence". It's the single biggest idea in this round and it's more build than a badge. Draft 2 and 3 show it; draft 1 has none because it parsed cleanly.
2
Should the drafts-waiting badge also appear on today? You chose a /recipes sub-page over a seventh nav slot, which means you only learn drafts exist by visiting /recipes — and today is the page you actually open.
3
Bulk accept — want it at all? Only direction B has it. It's the fastest path through a morning batch and also the easiest way to put something unreviewed into the catalog, which is what staging was meant to prevent.
4
How long does a draft live? Nothing here expires. If you don't review for three weeks, twenty drafts accumulate — fine in C, unpleasant in A. Auto-discard after N days, or let them pile up?
