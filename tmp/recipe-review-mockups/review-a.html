Direction A — notes

  • The premise: review means checking a draft against what it came from. The source sits pinned on the left — the cookbook photo, the raw dictation, the source page — while you edit on the right. It is the only direction where you can catch the agent misreading something.
  • One flag per draft, not per field (your call, 2026-09-11): "clean parse" or "needs a look", nothing finer. Per-field provenance was judged more build than it was worth — so the source panel is the only thing telling you where to look, which is precisely why this direction won.
  • Macros are left blank when the source doesn't state them (drafts 2 and 3) rather than estimated — your call from the quality-bar question. Draft 1 has them because the blog published them.
  • Costs: heaviest page of the three, and at 375px the source stacks above the draft so you scroll past it to get to the fields. One draft at a time, and drafts never expire — a month away means clicking through whatever accumulated.
  • A photographed source becomes the recipe's image (your call) — so a cookbook page ends up on the recipe card. A dictated recipe has no image at all and falls back to the tag-label square.
  • Would move into the design system: the sticky .decide-bar — generic enough that budgeter's uncategorized queue wants the same thing.
