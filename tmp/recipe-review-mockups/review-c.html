Direction C — notes

  • The premise: a mail client. The rail keeps the batch visible and your position stable, the pane holds one editable draft, and j/k plus ⌘↵ mean a clean batch is cleared without touching the mouse.
  • Scales past three. If you ever queue a stack of twenty photos on a Sunday, this is the only one of the three that still works — A becomes twenty page-loads and B becomes an endless scroll.
  • Source is collapsed by default behind a disclosure, so it's available but not competing for width. That's the compromise: you can check it, but you won't by default — the opposite of A.
  • Costs: a 288px rail holding three items is mostly empty space, and the edit column is narrower than in A or B, which the ingredient table feels. It's the right shape for a volume you don't have yet.
  • Would move into the design system: the rail/pane split is close to the .settings-pane shape already shipped — likely a variant of it rather than a new component.
